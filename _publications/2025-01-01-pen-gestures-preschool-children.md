We conducted a study of 53 children ages 3-5 who produced over 700 pen gestures across 14 shapes of varying difficulty. By comparing kinematic, pressure, and geometric features across age groups with t-tests and one-way ANOVA, we gain insight into how children's pen gestures develop with age.
