This paper applies the Equal Weighted Model, Monte Carlo simulation, and Sharpe ratio maximization to construct portfolios from five key pharmaceutical assets. Monte Carlo sampling yields a minimum-variance portfolio and a maximum-Sharpe-ratio portfolio, with NVS emerging as the largest component.
