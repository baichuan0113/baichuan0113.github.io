**09/2023 - 01/2024 · [GitHub](https://github.com/baichuan0113/Pseudo-LiDAR)**

The Pseudo-LiDAR project leverages stereo vision techniques with two simple cameras to recognize objects and calculate depth. Using Roboflow for data preprocessing and SIFT (Scale-Invariant Feature Transform) matching, the system processes stereo image pairs to identify objects and estimate their distances. This offers a cost-effective alternative to traditional LiDAR, providing depth perception and object recognition for a range of applications.
