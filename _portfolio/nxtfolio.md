**01/2023 - 05/2023 · [GitHub](https://github.com/baichuan0113/NXTFolio)**

NXTFolio is a visual "LinkedIn" for creative, visual, and fashion industry professionals, developed with Agile methodology in Ruby on Rails.

* Built a matching system that lets professionals upload visual portfolios and find collaborators based on specific job needs.
* Optimized search with custom sorting and filtering algorithms for more accurate keyword matching and display priority.
* Used the Cucumber testing framework (BDD) for comprehensive end-to-end testing.
