**12/2020 - 05/2021 · [PDF](/files/portfolio-construction-pharmaceutical-2021.pdf)**

This project uses the Equal Weighted Model, Monte Carlo simulation, and Sharpe ratio maximization to evaluate five key pharmaceutical assets. By applying the Monte Carlo method we constructed portfolios with minimum variance and maximum Sharpe ratio, revealing NVS as the largest component. The insights are useful for investors in the pharmaceutical sector.

Published as [Portfolio construction for pharmaceutical industry](/publication/2021-portfolio-construction-pharmaceutical) in E3S Web of Conferences (2021).
