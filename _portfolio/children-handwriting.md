**06/2024 - Present · Texas A&M University**

* Conducted a study of 53 children ages 3-5 with over 700 pen gestures across 14 shapes of varying difficulty.
* Compared kinematic, pressure, and geometric features across ages 3.5-7 using t-tests and one-way ANOVA.
* Gained insight into how children's pen gestures change as they develop.

This work led to the paper [Looking at the Difference in Pen Gestures of Preschool Age Children](/publication/2025-pen-gestures-preschool-children).
