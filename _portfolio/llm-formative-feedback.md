**08/2024 - Present · Utah State University, Computer Science Education Group**

This project studies how formative feedback generated by different large language models affects learning in an introductory programming course.

* Collected data from 48 CS1 students across 5 lab assignments.
* Designed customized prompts to compare learning gains and feedback alignment across 3 LLM models.
* Administered pre- and post-surveys to evaluate feedback quality and effectiveness.
* Performed ANOVA and group analysis to assess learning gains and the impact on student motivation.
