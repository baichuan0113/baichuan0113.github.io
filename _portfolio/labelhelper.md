**03/2024 - Present · [GitHub](https://github.com/baichuan0113/labelHelper)**

My master's thesis investigates the use of an OpenAI API chatbot to educate senior citizens on utilizing Roboflow for image labeling. The study assesses the chatbot's effectiveness in teaching, its efficiency in imparting knowledge, and the accuracy of image labeling performed by seniors after training. The goal is to bridge the digital divide by making advanced AI tools accessible and user-friendly for the elderly population.
