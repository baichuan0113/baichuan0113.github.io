Hi, I'm Baichuan Li, a Ph.D. student in Operations Research at [Southern Methodist University](https://www.smu.edu/lyle/departments/orem) (2025 - present), where I work as a graduate research and teaching assistant in the Department of Operations Research and Engineering Management. Before SMU, I received an M.S. in Computer Science from [Texas A&M University](https://engineering.tamu.edu/cse/) (2025), where I was a member of the [Sketch Recognition Lab](https://srltamu.com/) under the guidance of Prof. Tracy Hammond, and a B.S. in Mathematics from the [University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu/) (2022).

My research focuses on **large language models, Bayesian inference, and sequential decision making**. I am currently developing methods that combine Bayesian information seeking with LLM fine-tuning so that agents gather information and make decisions more effectively in multi-turn interactive settings. I also work on the reliability and evaluation of LLM agents, including memory commitment, verification gates, and benchmarks for tutoring and domain-specific NLP. My master's thesis at the [Sketch Recognition Lab](https://srltamu.com/) at Texas A&M studied the interpretability of LLM-generated feedback for image-labeling sketches.
